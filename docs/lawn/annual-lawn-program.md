# Annual Lawn Program

## Overview

This program is designed for a cool-season Colorado lawn with an emphasis on:

- Soil health
- Water conservation
- Drought tolerance
- Weed prevention
- Root development
- Long-term sustainability

The goal is continuous improvement over multiple growing seasons rather than pursuing a perfect lawn in a single year.

## Seasonal Priorities

### Spring

Primary Goals:

- Wake up the lawn
- Improve soil health
- Prevent crabgrass
- Tune irrigation
- Encourage healthy root growth

---

### Summer

Primary Goals:

- Maintain lawn health
- Reduce drought stress
- Continue soil improvement
- Avoid unnecessary stress

Focus on survival rather than rapid growth.

---

### Fall

Primary Goals:

- Eliminate perennial weeds
- Overseed
- Repair damage
- Strengthen root systems
- Prepare for winter dormancy

Fall is the primary renovation season.

---

### Winter

Primary Goals:

- Plan improvements
- Maintain equipment
- Review annual notes
- Prepare for next season

## Annual Goals

- Increase turf density.
- Reduce annual water usage.
- Improve soil organic matter.
- Reduce herbicide dependency.
- Improve drought tolerance.
- Expand preferred grass varieties through overseeding.
- Maintain healthy shrubs and trees.
- Continuously improve irrigation efficiency.

---

## Success Metrics

At the end of each season evaluate:

- Turf density
- Weed pressure
- Water consumption
- Irrigation performance
- Soil quality
- Root depth
- Bare spot recovery
- Overall curb appeal

Record observations in the annual property journal.
