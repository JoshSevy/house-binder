# Weed Control

## Philosophy

A healthy lawn is the primary weed prevention tool.

Focus on:

- Proper mowing
- Soil health
- Irrigation
- Density

Chemical control is a secondary tool.

---

## New Seeded Areas

Do not apply post-emergent herbicides until:

- Grass has been mowed at least 3 times

Preferred control:

- Hand pulling
- Spot removal

---

## Established Lawn

Common weeds:

- Dandelion
- Clover
- Plantain
- Thistle
- Spurge

Preferred treatment:

- Spot spray only

Avoid blanket applications unless necessary.

---

## Weed Prevention

Methods:

- Proper mowing height
- Adequate fertilization
- Deep watering
- Overseeding thin areas

---

## 2026 Notes

Delayed post-emergent applications to allow new seed to establish.

Hand pulled weeds in newly seeded areas.
