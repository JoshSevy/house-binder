# Insect Control

## Philosophy

Treat based on observed pest pressure.

Avoid unnecessary applications.

---

## Grubs

Observed:

- Found while raising sprinkler heads in spring 2026.

Monitoring:

- Brown patches
- Turf lifting easily
- Animal digging activity

---

## Treatment Options

## Preventative

- Chlorantraniliprole products

## Biological

- Beneficial nematodes

---

## Inspection Method

1. Cut 12" x 12" section
2. Lift turf
3. Count grubs

Guidelines:

- Less than 5: monitor
- 5-10: watch closely
- More than 10: consider treatment

---

## 2026 Notes

Initial grub discovery occurred during sprinkler maintenance.

No major grub damage observed.
