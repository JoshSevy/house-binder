# Lawn Renovation Playbook

## Purpose

Restore turf density, improve drought tolerance, reduce weed pressure, and establish new grass while improving long-term soil health.

---

# Best Timing

**Primary Renovation Window**

Late summer through early fall.

Colorado's cooler temperatures and warm soil provide the best conditions for establishing cool-season grasses.

---

# Preparation

## Assess the Lawn

- [ ] Photograph the lawn
- [ ] Identify thin areas
- [ ] Identify bare spots
- [ ] Identify weed pressure
- [ ] Verify irrigation coverage
- [ ] Inspect sprinkler heads

---

## Materials

### Seed

- Preferred drought-tolerant Colorado blend

### Soil

- Topsoil or compost
- Peat moss (optional)

### Fertility

- Fish Fertilizer
- Kelp
- Humic Acid
- Yucca Extract
- Liquid Iron (optional)

### Weed Control

- SpeedZone
- Mesotrione (during seeding)

### Equipment

- Backpack sprayer
- Core aerator
- Broadcast spreader
- Lawn roller (optional)
- Rake
- Hose

---

# Phase 1 - Weed Control

## Application 1

- Broadcast SpeedZone
- Record weather
- Photograph results

---

## Evaluation

Wait 2–3 weeks.

Questions:

- Did creeping Charlie decline?
- Did clover decline?
- Are additional applications needed?

---

## Application 2 (If Needed)

Repeat according to label directions.

---

# Phase 2 - Renovation

## Core Aeration

- Aerate entire lawn
- Make multiple passes on compacted soil

---

## Overseeding

- Apply preferred grass blend
- Increase rate in thin areas

---

## Topdress

Lightly cover seed.

Avoid burying seed too deeply.

---

## Mesotrione

Apply at seeding to reduce weed competition during establishment.

---

# Phase 3 - Establishment

## Watering

Keep seed consistently moist until germination.

Transition to deeper watering as roots develop.

---

## Fertility

Resume lawn fertilizer program after seedlings become established.

Continue:

- Fish
- Kelp
- Humic
- Yucca

---

# Phase 4 - First Mowing

- Mow high
- Sharp blade
- Avoid removing more than one-third of the blade

---

# Spring Follow-Up

## Pre-Emergent

Apply Prodiamine in areas that are **not** being newly seeded.

---

## Spot Repairs

Seed only areas that did not establish well.
