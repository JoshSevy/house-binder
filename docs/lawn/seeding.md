# Seeding and Overseeding Guide

## Philosophy

The goal is to establish strong roots before pursuing aggressive top growth.

Priority order:

1. Germination
2. Root development
3. Density
4. Appearance

---

## Spring Overseeding

Advantages:

- Cooler temperatures
- Increased moisture
- Longer growing season

Disadvantages:

- Weed pressure
- Summer heat arrives quickly

---

## Fall Overseeding

Preferred overseeding season.

Advantages:

- Warm soil
- Cool air temperatures
- Lower weed pressure
- Reduced water requirements

Target window:

- Late August through September

---

## New Seed Watering

## Germination Phase

Goal:

Keep top 0.5 to 1 inch of soil moist.

Typical schedule:

- Multiple light waterings daily
- Avoid puddling
- Avoid complete drying

---

## Establishment Phase

Begin reducing frequency.

Goal:

Encourage deeper roots.

---

## First Mowing

Requirements:

- Grass reaches mowing height
- Soil is firm enough to support mower traffic

Recommendations:

- Mow high
- Avoid sharp turns
- Minimize traffic

---

## Patch Repair Process

1. Remove dead material
2. Loosen soil
3. Add topsoil
4. Apply seed
5. Lightly rake
6. Water consistently

---

## Lessons Learned

## 2026

- Sunny areas dried significantly faster than shaded areas.
- Hand watering improved germination consistency.
- Spot seeding after initial establishment worked well.
