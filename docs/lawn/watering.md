# Watering Guide

## Current Water Restrictions

Denver Water recommendation:

- Water 2 days per week
- 3 cycles of 7 minutes each

Current Schedule:

- 3:00 AM
- 5:00 AM

Typical Runtime:

- 10 minutes
- Soak period
- 10 minutes

---

## Established Lawn

Target:

- Approximately 1 inch of water per week

Monitor:

- Footprint retention
- Crunchy grass
- Blue-gray coloration
- Dry spots

---

## New Seed

Goal:

- Keep surface consistently moist

Avoid:

- Allowing seed to dry out
- Standing water
- Excessively soggy soil

Transition to deeper watering after germination.

---

## Soil Moisture Test

Use screwdriver test.

Well-watered soil:

- Easy penetration to 4-6 inches

Dry soil:

- Difficult penetration below surface
