# Lawn Fertilizer Recipes

## Philosophy

The lawn program focuses on:

- Soil health
- Root development
- Water efficiency
- Drought tolerance
- Sustainable growth

Products Used:

- Neptune's Harvest Fish Fertilizer
- Neptune's Harvest Kelp
- Neptune's Harvest Humic Acid 12%
- Liquid Iron
- Unsulfured Molasses
- Yucca Extract
- Liquid Aeration

---

## Spring Blend

Apply every 3 weeks.

Per 1 Gallon Concentrate:

- 8 oz Fish Fertilizer
- 4 oz Kelp
- 3 oz Humic Acid
- 2 oz Liquid Iron
- 1 tbsp Molasses
- 1-2 oz Yucca Extract
- 2-4 oz Liquid Aeration
- Water to fill 1 gallon

Purpose:

- Spring green-up
- Root development
- Soil activation

---

## Summer Blend

Apply every 3 weeks.

Per 1 Gallon Concentrate:

- 6 oz Fish Fertilizer
- 5 oz Kelp
- 4 oz Humic Acid
- 2 oz Liquid Iron
- 1 tbsp Molasses
- 2 oz Yucca Extract
- 3 oz Liquid Aeration
- Water to fill 1 gallon

Purpose:

- Reduce heat stress
- Improve drought tolerance
- Increase water infiltration
- Continue building soil biology

Notes:

- Reduce fish fertilizer slightly during periods of prolonged heat.
- Increase kelp and humic acid to support stress recovery.
- Continue regular applications even if growth slows.

---

## Seedling Blend

Use after germination.

Per 1 Gallon Concentrate:

- 2 oz Fish Fertilizer
- 2 oz Kelp
- 1 oz Humic Acid
- 1 oz Yucca Extract
- Water to fill

Purpose:

- Gentle root development
- Seedling support

---

## Application Guidelines

- Apply during the cooler parts of the day.
- Avoid application during extreme heat (>90°F) unless irrigation is available.
- Shake concentrate well before use.
- Store concentrates out of direct sunlight.
- Clean sprayer thoroughly after each use.
