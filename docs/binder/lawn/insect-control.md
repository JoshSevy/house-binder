# Insect Control

## Philosophy

Treat based on observed pest pressure.

Avoid unnecessary applications.

Prioritize prevention, monitoring, and targeted treatments over routine blanket applications.

---

# Annual Strategy

## Spring (May–June)

- Inspect lawn for grub damage.
- Apply preventative chlorantraniliprole if grub populations have been observed in previous years.
- Continue annual applications for 2–3 years before reevaluating.

## Summer

Monitor for:

- Japanese beetles
- Grub damage
- Animal digging
- Turf stress

Record observations in the property journal.

## Fall

Inspect grub populations.

Evaluate whether preventative treatments should continue next season.

# Japanese Beetles

Observed:

- Moderate adult populations beginning in Summer 2026.

Current Plan:

- Monitor population annually.
- Continue preventative grub control for several seasons.
- Reevaluate after population declines.

Notes:

- Avoid placing pheromone traps near the property as they can attract additional beetles.
- Spot treat ornamentals only if damage becomes significant.

---

# Treatment Options

## Preventative

- Chlorantraniliprole products

Application Timing:

- Late spring / early summer

Purpose:

- Prevent young grubs from damaging turf roots.

---

## Biological

- Beneficial nematodes

Best Used:

- When soil temperatures are appropriate.
- As a long-term biological management option.

---

# Inspection Method

1. Cut a 12" × 12" section of turf.
2. Lift the turf carefully.
3. Count grubs within the root zone.

Guidelines:

- Less than 5: Monitor
- 5–10: Watch closely
- More than 10: Consider treatment
