# Shrubs and Landscape Beds

## Philosophy

Focus on root health and soil quality.

Avoid excessive nitrogen.

---

## Shrub Biostimulant Recipe

Per 1 Gallon:

- 2 oz Fish Fertilizer
- 2 oz Kelp
- 1-2 oz Humic Acid
- 1 oz Yucca Extract
- Water to fill

Apply every 4-6 weeks during active growth.

---

## Mulch

Target depth:

- 1 to 2 inches

Benefits:

- Moisture retention
- Weed suppression
- Soil improvement

---

## Watering

Water deeply and infrequently.

Encourage deep root development.
